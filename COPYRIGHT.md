## Automated access and search

Ordinary search indexing remains permitted by the crawler policy. The [robots.txt file](robots.txt) requests exclusion of this project's Pages content for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and CCBot. CCBot builds Common Crawl datasets; Google-Extended also controls certain Gemini grounding uses, not ordinary Google Search inclusion.

**Deployment location matters:** compliant crawlers read `https://cazzy-aporbo.github.io/robots.txt`. A copy at `https://cazzy-aporbo.github.io/Curious-Coder/robots.txt` is not the domain-root policy. The file in this repository is prepared for that root location and scopes its exclusions to `/Curious-Coder/`, without excluding other project sites on the same host. Merge it with any existing root policy rather than overwriting unrelated rules. Root deployment must be verified separately; publishing this repository alone does not establish it.

Robots directives are voluntary requests, not authentication, copy protection, or a way to force citation. They do not control GitHub-hosted source, forks, downloads, previously collected content, or crawlers that ignore them. Allowing search indexing does not grant general reuse or model-training permission for original material.

Crawler documentation: [OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots), [Anthropic](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler), [Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/google-common-crawlers#google-extended), and [Common Crawl](https://commoncrawl.org/ccbot).
